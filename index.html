02 / Research Projects

Selected research work

A selection of research implementations covering multimodal AI, reliability, computer vision, and robotics.

01

HALP-Bench: Pre-Generation Hallucination Probing for Vision-Language Models

A representation-probing study asking whether internal VLM representations can predict model-specific hallucination labels before answer generation. The implementation uses visual, vision-token, and query-token representations with leakage-aware image grouping and lightweight probes.

Vision-Language Models Hallucination Representation Probing Trustworthy AI
View repository ↗
02

ChartQAPro: Evidence-First Reasoning

A controlled comparison between direct chart question answering and an explicit evidence-first pipeline. The VLM first extracts structured chart-supported evidence and a second stage generates the final answer from that evidence representation.

Multimodal Reasoning Chart QA Evidence Extraction Evaluation
View repository ↗
03

Trustworthy Industrial Anomaly Detection Under Distribution Shift

A reliability-focused visual anomaly detection pipeline using frozen visual representations, k-nearest-neighbor anomaly scoring, post-hoc calibration, and controlled image corruptions to study performance and confidence under distribution shift.

Anomaly Detection Calibration Distribution Shift Computer Vision
View repository ↗
04

Uncertainty-Aware Underwater Semantic Segmentation

An underwater perception study combining DeepLabV3 with MobileNetV3-Large, Monte Carlo Dropout, and temperature scaling to examine segmentation quality, uncertainty, and confidence calibration.

Semantic Segmentation Uncertainty Calibration Underwater Robotics
View repository ↗
05

Pose-Based Fall Detection from Frozen Human Pose Estimation

A lightweight spatiotemporal fall-detection pipeline based on frozen YOLO11n-Pose features, 17-joint human skeleton sequences, motion descriptors, graph-based spatial modeling, temporal convolutions, and attention.

Human Pose Temporal Modeling Action Recognition Robotics
View repository ↗
06

DeepWeeds Multispecies Image Classification

A comparative computer-vision study of EfficientNet-B0, MobileNetV3-Large, and ViT-Tiny for nine-class weed recognition, using class-balanced training, grouped splitting, validation-driven selection, held-out testing, and error analysis.

Image Classification Transfer Learning Vision Transformers Model Evaluation

M.Sc. Thesis — Imitation Learning for Humanoid Robots

My thesis proposed a two-stage framework for learning humanoid-robot behaviors by combining self-supervised visual representation learning with reinforcement learning. The study focused on greeting and wiping skills for a NAO humanoid robot in simulation. ViT-B/16 and EfficientNetV2-S were evaluated within a time-contrastive learning setting, and the learned visual representations were then used to construct imitation rewards for reinforcement learning.
